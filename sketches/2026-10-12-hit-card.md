# The whole hit is the card

Milestone / step: after milestone 11's realignment (#447)
Date: 2026-10-12

## What I am about to do

Brian, 2026-10-12, after the section header: "I see the metadata is the
native fontsize which makes sense, but it is hard to pick out. What if
we made the metadata part of the chunk card as well as the paper name?"

So the card is the hit, not the passage. It has two regions:

- **a head**, on the page's ground so it reads as a band across the top
  of the card: the measures and the handle on one line, the document's
  title and its arrow, and the section with its `#`;
- **the passage**, on the card's raised surface, under a hairline.

The rule down the left, muted and the brand when open, is the whole
card's. The chevron sits on the card's bottom edge. Hits are separated
by space between cards rather than by a rule above each.

## How I expect it to work

`hits.html`: `<li class="hit">` holds `<div class="hit-head">`
(measures, heading, section) and the `.passage` as now, whose `.full`
loses its card styling and keeps its padding. CSS: the card's
declarations move from `.passage .full` to `.hit`; `.hit:has(details
[open])` turns the rule; `.hit-head` takes the ground, the hairline, and
the top corners; `.passage-section` loses its negative margins. The foot
hangs below the card by half the button's height.

## What I expect to be uncertain or difficult

- `.hit`'s `@starting-style` and transition stay on the one rule (#425).
- The chevron overlapping the card's border: the card must not clip.
- The group head's "first hit has no top rule" becomes dead and goes.
- Tests that name `.passage .full` as the card and the section as the
  passage's first child change to the new structure.

## What actually happened that I did not expect
- Nothing surprising in the build: the card's declarations moved from
  `.passage .full` to `.hit` whole, and the bare-class guard (#425) was
  satisfied by adding the border transition to `.hit`'s one rule.
- The header-only chunk (#456) is now the head alone: its empty `.full`
  is not displayed and the head loses its hairline and takes all four
  corners.
- The chevron hangs 0.85rem below the card, inside the 1.1rem gap
  between cards, so it never touches the next one; measured in the
  screenshot in both themes.
- Injections: five, all caught.
